Why structured this way

One paid step. One free-if-it-fails step.

The audit carries a fee because it takes real time on-site and produces a concrete deliverable regardless of what happens next — that work has value whether or not you act on it.

The improvement work is priced differently on purpose. I want to be judged on results, not hours booked. If I lead a project and the savings don't materialise, you don't pay for my time — that risk sits with me, not you.
